The Founder's Journey

A passion for botanical science, feminine empowerment, and timeless aesthetic perfection.

Meet The Founder

"Beauty Should Nurture The Soul As Much As The Skin."

ZYRA was founded with a singular ambition: to bridge the gap between high-fashion luxury makeup and uncompromising clean ingredient safety.

Disillusioned by traditional beauty brands filled with hidden parabens and synthetic fillers, our founder partnered with master cosmetic chemists to create weightless formulas enriched with pure Damask Rose water, hyaluronic acid, and botanical oils.

"ZYRA is not just a brand; it is a celebration of soft feminine power and self-love."

Brand Philosophy

We believe every individual deserves cosmetics that feel weightless, look regal, and actively improve skin texture over time.

Future Goals

Expanding our sustainable refillable Rose Gold packaging initiative to reduce plastic waste by 80% by 2028.

Empowerment Initiative

5% of all ZYRA proceeds directly fund educational scholarships for young women in STEM and cosmetic science.
